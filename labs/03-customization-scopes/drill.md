The coach plays both team leads from the brief: Dana (Platform) and Jonas (Trailhead). It asks one question at a time, names who is speaking, times every answer with the shell, stays in character and keeps all feedback for the written report at the end.

### 60-second framing

Headline first, then one reason, then the proof. Stems you may use:

- "You will get one checklist, and Trailhead keeps… because…"
- "Each rule lives in… so that…"
- "You will see it working when the resolver shows…"

Sinhala-first note: resist building up to the point ("because the staging incident, because two teams, therefore…"). Say the layout first, then why.

### Think-aloud checkpoints (T+10 and T+45)

At T+10 and again at T+45 the coach asks what you are looking at and what you will check next. Answer with the four verbs: *I see… I think… I'll try… I'll check…*. Read a file path aloud slowly, segment by segment ("services, slash, api…"). If you need to read resolver output, say "Give me ten seconds to read this" instead of going silent.

### Three-minute update to both team leads

Skeleton: **Outcome → Evidence → Risk → Next action → Ask.** Address each lead by name at least once and give each one the sentence that answers their own condition. One number or file per sentence. Either lead may interrupt twice in total with a short question; answer it in one sentence and return to the skeleton.

### Objection round

Two objections in character, 90 seconds each, one technical and one commercial; the coach may ask one follow-up if your answer skips the evidence. Reply pattern: acknowledge → boundary → evidence → offer. The objections are not printed here — that is the point.
