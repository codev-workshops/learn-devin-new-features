This lab uses the four-part articulation drill with two think-aloud checkpoints. The coach plays the Kadé finance controller: polite, numerate, suspicious of any figure without a source, and impatient with product vocabulary.

### 60-second framing

Say what finance gets before you say how. Stems you may use:

- "Two people were blocked this morning for two different reasons: …"
- "There are two kinds of cap, and they do different jobs: …"
- "I am using … because…"
- "You will know it works when…"

Sinhala-first note: open with the outcome. Do not explain the contract, the three organizations and the tier list before you say what changes.

### Think-aloud checkpoint (T+10)

The coach asks what you are looking at and what you will check next. Use the four verbs: *I see… I think… I'll try… I'll check…*. Name the meter you read first and its number. If you need to read the simulator output, say "Give me ten seconds to read this".

### Think-aloud checkpoint (T+40)

You are filling in the numbers ledger. Say, for two numbers on your screen, what each one counts and what it leaves out. One number per sentence. End on a full stop, not on "…right?".

### Update to the finance controller (3 minutes)

Skeleton: **Outcome → Evidence → Risk → Next action → Ask.** Finance asked for a short update: choose your numbers carefully, say where each one comes from, and do not read the ledger aloud. Close with the decision you need from finance.

### Objection round

The coach raises one commercial and one technical objection, in character, 90 seconds each. Reply pattern: acknowledge → boundary → evidence → offer. The objections are not printed here — that is the point.
