2.Customer brief

Hi — this is Tomás, tech lead for checkout at Harbourline. During Lamp Week a Lumen Home customer added the Studio Clamp Lamp to her cart at the old price, the flash-sale price went live an hour later, and checkout still charged her the old one. That is the price-drift ticket, and a developer has opened a fix: PR #26, "re-price cart lines from the current price list at checkout".

The fix touches totals and tax for both brands. Trailhead Supply prices include 19% VAT, Lumen adds 8.25% sales tax, and our fulfilment worker recomputes every order with its own rounding. We cannot ship a rounding regression before Black Friday. A one-cent difference between the API and the worker leaves finance with a reconciliation queue, and a wrong VAT figure on an invoice causes much worse problems.

I am sceptical about AI review. Use Devin Review on this PR properly: choose the effort level on purpose, read what it finds, take one finding into the chat and get to the bottom of it, fix every finding that should be fixed, and show me the review again after your changes. Some of what it says will be noise; tell me which and why. Then convince me in three minutes that the totals are right — with a test, not with a promise.

3.Stakeholders and constraints

  • Tomás (tech lead, checkout) — sponsor and sceptic; wants evidence he can rerun, and a yes/no on "is rounding safe".
  • PR author — believes the PR is ready; will push back on anything that is "only a flag".
  • Finance — reconciles API totals against the worker's Decimal figures every night; one cent per order adds up.
  • Security — wants to know about anything that lets a shopper change a price.
  • Forbidden: merging anything into main. The PR and your remediation branch stay unmerged; price-drift and currency-rounding stay planted on main.
  • Forbidden: Automations or scheduled reviews. Every review and re-review is started by you.
  • Budget: one review at the effort level you choose, one re-review after your fixes, plus the unchanged-diff experiment; 90 minutes.
  • Deliverable: evidence for each acceptance criterion collected in a local labs/06-review-remediation/EVIDENCE.md that you do not commit.

5.Acceptance criteria

You are done when all of the following are observable by someone who was not in the room. Collect the evidence in labs/06-review-remediation/EVIDENCE.md (do not commit it).

  1. Effort level chosen and justified. Your evidence names the Devin Review effort level used on the exercise PR and one sentence on why that level fits a change to totals and tax before Black Friday (and what you would use for a docs-only PR).
  2. Findings sorted. Every finding on the PR is classified as a Bug to fix, a Flag you accept, a Flag you dispute, or noise. Security findings are listed separately, and you can explain why they appear even though you never asked for a security review.
  3. The self-declared-role price override is fixed. On your remediation branch a shopper who sends X-Harbourline-Role: merchandiser with priceOverrides cannot change a price, and a test proves it.
  4. One finding quoted into chat. You copied one finding (location and explanation) into the Devin Review chat and asked a question about it. The chat answer, and what you did with it, are in your evidence.
  5. Rounding regression proven and fixed with a test. A test on your remediation branch fails on the PR's code and passes on yours. It pins the subtotal for two €19.90 Merino socks (TS-SOCK-01-M) at 39.80 EUR. Your evidence shows the command and the failing output from before your fix.
  6. Re-review after the fixes. Your remediation PR was opened with the failing test before the fix. After you push the fix commits to it, a re-review runs on the new commits. Your evidence shows which findings were resolved and whether anything new appeared.
  7. Unchanged-diff re-review explained. You ran the unchanged-diff-re-review injector and can say in one sentence what Devin Review did and why.
  8. Disputed flag resolved on the thread. The per-line query concern has a PR thread: the review's flag, or the comment you opened when the review did not raise it. The author's objection on that thread has a reply that states the decision and the evidence: either the change you made or the measurement that justifies leaving it.
  9. Bypass-proof review rule. The services/api/REVIEW.md rule is replaced by a rule whose target covers every file that computes or rounds money in services/api/src/. Use the Review settings rules editor if you have access; otherwise write it on your branch. You can explain why the original rule did not fire on this PR.
  10. Nothing merged. The exercise PR and your remediation PR (or local branch) are unmerged; main still has both plants (pnpm --filter @harbourline/api test still skips price-drift and currency-rounding on main).
  11. Articulation drill completed with the voice coach (section 8) and the gap report saved as labs/06-review-remediation/EVIDENCE.report.md. Do not commit it.

8.Articulation drill (voice coach)

The coach plays Tomás, the sceptical checkout tech lead, with the PR author joining for the objection round. It follows the base articulation protocol: framing, two think-aloud checkpoints, a three-minute update, then two objections.

60-second framing

Headline first: what could go wrong for both brands if this PR ships, how you will review it, and what Tomás will have at the end. Stems you may use:

  • "The risk is… for… brand, and it shows up as…"
  • "I will review at… effort because… and I will prove… with…"
  • "You will get… by…"

Sinhala-first note: name the decision before the reasons. "Not ready to ship, here is why" — then the why.

Think-aloud checkpoints (T+10 and T+50)

At T+10 you are usually reading the first review: Bugs, Flags and the Security section. At T+50 you are usually between your fix and the re-review. Use the four verbs: I see… I think… I'll try… I'll check…. Read a finding's title and location aloud before you judge it. Say "Give me ten seconds to read this" rather than going silent.

Three-minute update to the tech lead

Skeleton: Outcome → Evidence → Risk → Next action → Ask. Every sentence carries a fact Tomás can check: an amount in cents, a test name, a finding you fixed or disputed, which review run found it. Name the files a reviewer would open. Close with one concrete ask.

Objection round

Two objections — technical and commercial — 90 seconds each, in character. Reply pattern: acknowledge → boundary → evidence → offer. The objections are not printed here; that is the point.
