The coach plays Harbourline's CISO. She is fluent in risk and audit, not in Devin settings, and she wants each sentence to be something she can repeat to her committee.

### 60-second framing

Lead with what is now true for every session, then the one word that makes it true, then what you still need from her. Stems you can use:

- "From today every Devin session can reach…"
- "What makes that stick is…"
- "What I need from you is…"

Sinhala-first note: say the outcome first — two places, nothing else — before naming any setting or level.

### Think-aloud checkpoint (T+10)

The coach asks what you are looking at and what you will check next. Answer with *I see… I think… I'll try… I'll check…*. At T+10 you should be deciding which list each destination goes into and at which level each profile sits, not polishing JSON.

### Think-aloud checkpoint (T+45)

Same four verbs, later in the block. The CISO has just changed something. Name what a session that is already running does about it, and how you checked. If you need to read the resolver output, say "Give me ten seconds to read the resolver" rather than going silent.

### Three-minute CISO update

Skeleton: **What is enforced → Where it is set → Who can loosen it (nobody / whom) → What runs stricter → When changes land → What it does not cover → Ask.** One fact per sentence. Name hosts, levels and profile names, not features. Pause after each heading word.

### Objection round

The CISO raises one technical and one commercial objection, in character, about 90 seconds each. Reply with: acknowledge → boundary → evidence → offer. The commercial objection needs a number — of hosts, packages or builds. The objections themselves are not printed here.
