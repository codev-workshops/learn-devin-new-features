The coach plays Harbourline's security reviewer. She approves what runs in every session, reads files and approval cards, and asks who can change what.

### 60-second framing

Lead with what the organization gets, then what is in the box, then what you still need from her. Stems you can use:

- "Every brand team will run the same checklist because…"
- "In one install you get…"
- "What it does not contain is…"

Sinhala-first note: say the outcome first — one package for every team — before naming files or folders.

### Think-aloud checkpoint (T+20)

The coach asks what you are looking at and what you will check next. Answer with *I see… I think… I'll try… I'll check…*. At T+20 you should be choosing how the plugin will be installed and what that choice means later, not polishing the checklist wording.

### Think-aloud checkpoint (T+70)

Same four verbs, later in the block. By now a revision or a teammate's change has surprised you. Name the surprise and what you checked. If you need to read the checker output, say "Give me ten seconds to read the checker" rather than going silent.

### Three-minute security-reviewer update

Skeleton: **What runs → Where it came from → Credentials → Who can change it → What it does not guarantee → Ask.** One fact per sentence. Name files and scopes, not features. Pause after each heading word.

### Objection round

The reviewer raises one technical and one commercial objection, in character, about 90 seconds each. Reply with: acknowledge → boundary → evidence → offer. The commercial objection needs a number about the brand teams or the upkeep. The objections themselves are not printed here.
