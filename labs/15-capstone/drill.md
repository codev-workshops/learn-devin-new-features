The capstone drill has no scaffold. Nothing on this page tells you what to say; the envelope, the clock and the customer do.

Start the coach yourself at T+0: in the Devin web app, select this repo in Agent mode, type `@skills:coach-15-capstone <your-handle>` and press the voice-call button. A facilitator running a cohort may add `--scenario <id>` to that line; otherwise the coach picks an envelope you have not drawn before. Keep the call connected for the whole half day; mute while you work (space to talk).

### 90-second framing

Right after the envelope. The customer stops talking and the clock starts.

### Think-aloud checkpoints (T+30, T+90)

Forty-five seconds each, whenever the coach calls in. The T+90 checkpoint comes after the customer has changed something.

### Four-minute customer update

Unscripted. No skeleton is printed here.

### Objection round

Three objections, ninety seconds each, each with one follow-up if your answer skips evidence.

### About the feedback

The coach grades from the speech transcript in the session history, not from audio. Every clarity and pronunciation line in its report is **transcript-based, approximate**; timings are ±5 s.
