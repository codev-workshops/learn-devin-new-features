## Customer brief

> **From:** Amara Okafor, COO, Harbourline
> **To:** The Devin lead on duty today
> **Subject:** One morning, three problems, no script
>
> Every week someone at Harbourline brings three things to the same stand-up: something broke last night, a brand team wants something new by a date, and legal, finance or security adds a rule we did not have last week. Today that someone is you, and I want to see how you run it with Devin.
>
> You will get one sealed envelope. Inside is a real-shaped morning for one of our brands: an incident that is costing us orders or money right now, a feature a brand lead has promised to a customer, and a constraint you must not break while you do either. Nobody will tell you which Devin capabilities to use. Choosing them, and saying why, is the job.
>
> By the end of the half day I want the incident understood and contained, the feature started the right way, the constraint respected and provable, and an update I could forward to the board without editing. Things will change while you work. That is also the job.

## Stakeholders and constraints

- **Amara Okafor (COO)** — owns the outcome and the budget for the day; she will ask what it cost.
- **The customer in the envelope** — a brand lead, a finance owner or a risk owner; the coach plays them on the call.
- **Your facilitator** — hands out the envelope when there is no coach call, and answers only "is this in scope?" questions.
- **Constraints**
  - The envelope is read to you once. Do not open `labs/15-capstone/scenarios/`; a learner who has read a scenario file draws a different one.
  - No scaffold: this page offers no stems, no feature list and no plan. The hidden answer is for the debrief, after the coach report.
  - Work on your own leaf branch. Planted defects stay on `main`; anything you fix is a pull request you close without merging.
  - Never ask anyone for credentials. Where a live integration is missing, use the repo's fixtures and say so.
  - Spend like it is your own money: the COO will ask for the session count and ACU you used.
  - Coaching is started by you, manually, at T+0.
